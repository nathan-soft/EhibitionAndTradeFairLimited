Who We Are

Superior Services in International Promotion.

Located in Lagos – Nigeria, Exhibition and Trade Fair Limited (ETL) provides superior services in managing and promotion of international trade fairs.

We are the official representative of Messe Frankfurt Exhibition, Germany in West Africa, representing Nigeria, Ghana, Cameroon, Benin, Togo, Gambia, Sierra Leone and Liberia.

With tailor-made customer focused services that meet customer’s needs in terms of location, budget, time and value for a given fair, we are your right contact to link you with the best trade fair that will suite your purpose.
